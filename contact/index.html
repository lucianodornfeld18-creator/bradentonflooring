Estimate Questions

Before you reach out — quick answers

How long does it take to receive my written flooring estimate?

You'll have the complete line-item document in your inbox within 24 hours of the in-home measurement. Most measurement visits themselves are scheduled within two to four business days of your first call, text or form submission, depending on your location within our 50-mile service radius.

Is the estimate genuinely free, or is there a visit charge?

It is genuinely free — no trip fee, no measurement charge, and no deposit required to receive pricing. The visit includes room measurements plus a quick slab flatness and moisture check, because accurate prep pricing is impossible without them. You are under no obligation to book after receiving the quote.

What information should I have ready before my estimate visit?

Helpful but not required: approximate square footage or room count, the floor type you're considering, whether old flooring needs removal, and any HOA or condo association flooring rules that apply to your building. If you don't have your association's flooring addendum handy, we can help you request the right document from your property manager.

Do you come out to condos and HOA-governed buildings for estimates?

Yes — condos and HOA communities are a large part of our work in Bradenton, Sarasota, Longboat Key and Anna Maria Island. During the visit we note the building's acoustic underlayment requirements, elevator and work-hour rules, and insurance certificate needs, and we build the association paperwork into the estimate timeline.
